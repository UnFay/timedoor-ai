1. AI is the concept of a machine or a program mimicking human behaviour and decision-making. Machine learning is the method by which AI systems are developed.
2. Machine learning is the training of AI systems to do a certain task, such as identifying objects in pictures, by feeding the AI system data in order for it to find patterns to recognize.
3. Supervised learning involves training the AI with data that has already been categorized and the expected outcome from certain inputs has already been decided. Unsupervised learning gives the AI uncategorized training data
